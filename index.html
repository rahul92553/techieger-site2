Questions we hear often

How long does a project take?

A simple business website or landing page can often be ready within a few days to two weeks. Apps and custom software take longer. We give you a timeline when we agree the scope.

What do I need to give you to start?

Just your idea, your business name and any logo or content you already have. If you are unsure, we help you decide what to include.

Will I own the website or software?

Yes. We will confirm ownership of the finished work with you in writing before we begin.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, hosting, bug fixing, security updates and technical support.

Can I get a quote before I commit?

Yes. The consultation is free, and you get a clear quote before any work starts.

Security built into every project

Our security checklist covers the basics that keep your site, your data and your customers safe.

Authentication & access

Require appropriate authentication and authorization.

Trusted origins only

Configure CORS to allow only trusted origins.

Security headers

Configure CSP, HSTS and other suitable headers.

Debug mode off

Set production debug mode to false.

No exposed files

Avoid publishing .env files, private keys and backups.

Secure database

Use least-privilege accounts and encrypted connections.

Hashed passwords

Use Argon2id or bcrypt, never plain-text storage.

Full security audit

Review the application, infrastructure and dependencies.
